Applied Data Cleaning & Quality Rules

Strict Audit Policy: Student records are verified against domain-valid boundaries. Imputation is conducted with leakage-free median and MICE strategies without dropping valid students:

  • 0.0 ≤ CGPA ≤ 10.0 — Eliminates corrupted GPA scale entries
  • 0.0 ≤ CodingTestScore ≤ 100.0 — Validates assessment rubric limits
  • 0.0 ≤ AptitudeTestScore ≤ 100.0 — Validates analytical test limits
  • 1.0 ≤ SoftSkillsRating ≤ 5.0 — Bounds interviewer rating scale
  • AttendancePercent ≥ 0.0 — Filters non-negative attendance metrics
  • drop_duplicates(subset=['CGPA', 'CodingTestScore', 'Stream']) — Audits conflicting duplicate profiles
